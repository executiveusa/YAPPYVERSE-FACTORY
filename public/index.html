The YAPPYVERSE Factory is the production system for turning approved character artwork into organized, reusable assets for cinematic scenes, animation, audio, comics, collectibles, and the ScrollVerse experience.

Canon first. Every approved character keeps a traceable identity, reference set, production status, and revision history.
