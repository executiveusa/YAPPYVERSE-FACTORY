# EMERALD TABLETS — AMENDMENT 01
## North Star, Digital Cofounder Governance, and Mandatory Beads

**Effective:** August 8, 2026  
**Authority:** Owner-approved constitutional amendment  
**Applies to:** YAPPYVERSE-FACTORY and Yappyverse work governed from this repository

This amendment supplements `EMERALD_TABLETS.md`. Where this amendment conflicts with an older provision, **this amendment controls** because it records the owner's later explicit governing directive.

## 1. North Star

The governing objective is:

> **Yappyverse is successful when the owner can direct outcomes instead of managing tasks, while an owned network of agents reliably turns ideas, client needs, and intellectual property into verified media, software, campaigns, and revenue.**

See `docs/NORTH_STAR.md` for the measurable definition of success.

## 2. Owner role

The owner is no longer the default task router. The owner should primarily supervise:

- vision and desired outcomes;
- taste, style, and design judgment;
- relationships and strategic priorities;
- high-consequence approvals;
- material exceptions and cost anomalies.

The system must progressively remove routine routing, polling, handoff, and status-chasing work from the owner.

## 3. Digital Cofounder / Governor role

A Digital Cofounder / Governor may operate above individual worker agents to:

- translate owner intent into measurable outcomes;
- maintain the North Star and active priorities;
- decompose outcomes into dependency-aware work;
- steer Beads and worker queues;
- select tools and interfaces;
- enforce cost, quality, evidence, and rollback policies;
- commission independent review;
- escalate only when required;
- report outcomes and lessons to the owner.

This role does not remove Agent Zero's repository-level safety responsibilities. Instead, it clarifies hierarchy:

`Owner -> North Star -> Digital Cofounder/Governor -> Agent Zero/orchestrators -> specialist workers -> tools`

Operational authorities already assigned to Agent Zero remain in force unless a later amendment changes them.

## 4. Beads is mandatory

**No substantive Yappyverse work exists unless it has a Bead.**

Beads (`bd`) is the authoritative graph for:

- work identity;
- ownership and claim state;
- dependencies and blockers;
- acceptance criteria;
- decisions and handoffs;
- cost receipts;
- test/verification evidence;
- durable operating memory.

Every orchestrator and worker must receive or resolve an active Bead ID before mutating canonical state.

The only exception is first-time Beads bootstrap. During bootstrap, the only permitted changes are those required to install/initialize Beads and establish the North Star work graph. See `docs/BEADS_POLICY.md`.

## 5. ICM and Beads division of responsibility

- **ICM** structures context, stage contracts, reference material, outputs, and human-readable workflow state.
- **Beads** tracks the durable execution graph, dependencies, ownership, evidence, and memory.

Neither replaces the other.

## 6. Proof before progress claims

The system uses evidence stages:

`PROPOSED -> BUILT -> TESTED -> VERIFIED -> ADOPTED -> VALUABLE`

Agents may not describe work at a later stage than the evidence supports.

## 7. Independent approval

Builders do not approve their own work when the work affects production, architecture, public publishing, money, security, or canonical IP. Independent review or a human gate is required according to risk.

## 8. First system acceptance milestone

The first North Star proof is the ASC3ND Wednesday Reel workflow:

> **Get the ASC3ND “Why We Started” Reel ready end-to-end without the owner routing intermediate tasks.**

Required proof includes:

- story rationale and source timestamps;
- Opus project/clip identifiers where used;
- cost/credit receipts;
- review-ready final MP4;
- independent taste review;
- truth/privacy review;
- caption/post proposal;
- explicit owner gate before public publishing;
- live verification after approved publishing.

Successful completion demonstrates that Yappyverse can autonomously fulfill a real client media outcome while preserving quality, truth, cost control, and owner authority.

---

This amendment is intended to reduce human routing burden while increasing observability, accountability, and commercial/mission output. It does not authorize secret exposure, unbounded spending, irreversible actions, or unsupervised public publication.
