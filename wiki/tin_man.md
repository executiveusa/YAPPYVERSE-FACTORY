## Tin Man (music arc)
- Soulful lead singer of BLACK AMERICA — a band parodying America (the band): the shirts/logo use the exact America font/curve style but read "BLACK AMERICA". Mixed-race descendants playing together; the joke is everyone swore they were a Black soul band before hearing them (Hall & Oates / America / Terry Kath friendship energy).
- Vegas 2056 park concerts; neon teal mic, holographic keytar, chrome sax with red neon stripes, transparent neon guitar. Easter egg: "Hall & Oates Memorial Lawn" hologram.

## Thin Man (noir arc)
- Pauli's enforcer: "the man without a heart who keeps Pauli's empire clean." Ruthless, respected, always in sharp tailored suits; tall gaunt silhouette, blood-red tie as the single color pop, shadowed eyes.
- CANON TWIST: surface = heartless; hidden core = the most loyal, compassionate, protective of them all — loyalty is his love language. Moral irony: everyone calls him heartless but he risks everything when it matters.
- Easter eggs: distant flickering "Oz" neon sign; emerald-stitched trench-coat lining; graffiti/hologram lyric "Oz never did give nothing to the Tin Man…".
