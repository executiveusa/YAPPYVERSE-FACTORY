# RHAH & DUO — Character Summary (v1, pending Bambu approval)
Source chats: "Ezri Spellstrike Character Design" (ezri-rhah-duo.txt, 17 msgs). Original working name "Ezri/Ezra" was RENAMED to Rhah. Related chats still to extract: "duo and bigsaw", "Duo character walkthrough".

## RHAH (main character)
- Formerly "Ezra"; codename "Ra", spelled R-H-A-H. World Breaker #33. Threat level: CALAMITY.
- A living sun with unlimited energy; needs little sleep, chooses none. Mischievous frown, glint in his eye.
- Powers (locked annotations): Spirit Manipulation; Zero-Point Movement; Beast-Link Telepathy; Elemental Memory (Fire-Water duality); Bone Recode Reflexes; Planet Echo (hears the planet's whispers).
- Art lock: pencil manga sketch, faithful to original artist's drawing; manga right-to-left layout; Ghibli-ink textures over Mustang Maxx visual base ("Yappyverse Noir-Cel Fusion"). NO RELATION between Maxx, Duo, Rhah (style base only).
- Arc: 12-ISSUE SOLO SERIES before meeting any other Yappyverse characters. Must conquer Planet Echo Phobos alone; issues 11-12 = spaceship to Earth (the blue planet) to assist other Yappyverse people.
- Fears: SPACE CANDY (monster, undefeated). Koi fish = symbol of hope, visions, messages from earth.
- One Piece-style cameo character (stitch, hand on hat) pops up at humorous moments.

## DUO (the Balancer)
- Sole original being of Planet Echo Phobos. Blue headband, ONE EYE. The ONLY full-color element in Rhah's book.
- Role: Rhah's conscience / inner voice — balanced, beyond judgment and bias. Philosophy = Cherokee "Two Wolves" story (user-supplied PDF: two-wolves-cherokee-story-dos lobos theme -planet of the wolves.pdf). Opening scene: DUO tells Two Wolves to Rhah ("You don't fight him. You starve him. You feed the one you want to win.").
- Backstory (2056, post-war, Arizona Southwest desert): spirit "Duel" born by the Chiefs of the Southwest desert; they tried to contain him; man's greed empowered and freed him. He stays on the planet as a MIRROR — people arrive to torture themselves with their own thoughts/emotions until they take control of their lives. Helpful, but painted as a villain. Horror-style cover treatment.
- Soundtrack direction: Arizona Southwest desert feel.

## PLANET ECHO PHOBOS ("The Mirror World")
- Training realm in the Yappyverse multistrata, invisible to most. Terrain responds to emotional patterns (ground breathes when souls lie, sky darkens with fear, time loops around trauma until reframed). Orbit: outside the Eye Nebula, stabilized by conscience energy. Surface: jagged deserts, floating islands, biome-shifting caves. Inhabitants: DUO; Spirits of the Trials (fear-forged monsters); Sentient Koi Streams; Ember Satellites (ancient observers).
- ISSUE 1 "The One You Feed" (The Spellstrike Journals) LOCKED for production.
