# YAPPYVERSE Product District

**The offer: "Want your own Yappyverse? We build it for you."**

The Yappyverse is the world. Pauli's Place is a place inside it. The Product District is how the world pays for itself: done-for-you character universes, built with the same factory that built this one.

## What we sell
1. **Character creation** - original characters with locked visual identity: character card, style-lock reference, palette/linework rules, registry entry. Proven by this repo: 50+ characters, naming convention `{canonical_id}_{category}_{variant}_v{NNN}`, versioned registry.
2. **World canon** - wiki, relationship map, scene modules, world rules (sepia-noir Vegas 2056 and beyond). Governance included: NORTH STAR, Emerald Tablets, amendment process.
3. **Story production** - comics (panel-lock pipeline: identity lock, master prompts, trauma-drive-humor formula), anime shot grammar, image-to-video scene specs (Veo 3 SceneSpec XML, Marble 3D world prompts).
4. **The factory itself** - MAS-Factory pipeline: extraction, summarization, wiki, asset registry, image naming and versioning. What mined 97 chats into a governed canon in one day is the product.

## Proof points (all in this repo)
- 50+ governed characters in `assets/registry.json`, versioned releases (v1.2.0 -> v1.3.0).
- `wiki/` character entries with origin, card, and canon scene modules.
- Production pipelines captured as canon: comic automation, ARCHITECT character-card protocol (<8k chars, repeatable), JSON/Drive + MCP asset pattern.
- Live homepage: https://yappyverse-factory.vercel.app

## Boundaries
- Yappyverse is the personal portfolio universe. It is NOT New World Kids (nonprofit); the two never mix canon, branding, or money.
- Adult-market material (e.g. Crypto Cuties line) stays out of the family canon and ships as a separate package only on his explicit word.
- Nothing here is sold, published, or promised to a customer without the owner's approval.
